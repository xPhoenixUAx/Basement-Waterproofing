About cookies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. This page describes the cookies and browser storage used by the baseline belowline website, operated by YOUR COMPANY NAME.

Essential session cookie

When you prepare or submit the contact form, the PHP endpoint uses the belowline_session cookie to maintain a form session. It supports security-token checks and helps identify repeated submissions from the same session. The cookie contains a session identifier rather than the request description.

The cookie is configured as HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax. On the HTTPS production site it is also configured as Secure. It is a session cookie; browser session-restoration behavior may affect when it is removed.

Technology Purpose Duration
belowline_session cookie Form security and duplicate-request handling Browser session
Server-side accepted-request fingerprints Identify retries within the same session Up to one hour, subject to earlier session expiry
Temporary rate-limit counters Limit repeated form attempts Ten-minute attempt window; expired files cleaned periodically

The latter two records are server-side records, not additional browser cookies.

Browser storage

The baseline form does not save your name, email address or project description to localStorage or sessionStorage. Unsaved form details remain in the current page and may be lost when it is reloaded or closed. Browser autofill or form restoration is controlled separately by your browser.

A service-page link may include a service category in the URL so that the main form can select it. That URL does not include your contact details or project description.

Optional technologies

The baseline website does not include optional analytics cookies, advertising pixels or embedded third-party videos. Local fonts and images can be served from the website itself. If optional third-party services are added, this page and any required preference controls need to reflect their actual behavior before those services are activated.

Your choices

You can use browser controls to remove or restrict cookies. Blocking the session cookie may prevent the contact form from working correctly. You may contact the operator directly by email if you prefer not to use the form.

This baseline site has no optional-cookie preference banner because no optional cookie technologies are included. That implementation must be reassessed if the deployed website adds such technologies.

Contact

For questions about cookies or browser storage, contact hello@example.com. More information about submitted request data is available in the Privacy Policy.
