About this website

This Privacy Policy explains how belowline, operated by YOUR COMPANY NAME, handles information collected through this website. You can contact us at hello@example.com or YOUR BUSINESS ADDRESS about privacy questions.

The website helps users explore basement waterproofing services and submit inquiries. It does not perform or supervise work carried out by independent providers.

Information you provide

When you submit a request, we collect your name, email address, ZIP code, selected service, project description and privacy acknowledgement. If you select a location in the website’s moisture explorer, that selection is also included in the request.

Please provide only information relevant to your inquiry. Do not include payment information, identification documents, entry codes or other sensitive information in the description. The form does not request a telephone number or accept file uploads.

Technical information

The website and its hosting services process technical information needed to deliver pages and protect the form, such as connection information and request logs. The form uses a session cookie for security and duplicate-request handling. See the Cookie Policy for details.

The baseline website does not include advertising pixels or optional analytics. If additional technologies are introduced, this policy and any relevant user choices must be updated to reflect them.

How information is used

We use submitted information to review and respond to your inquiry, clarify the project, discuss possible next steps and protect the website from misuse. Request details are delivered to the operator’s configured email address.

Submitting a request does not mean that an appointment, estimate or service has been arranged.

Service providers and sharing

Hosting, email and other service providers may process information as needed to operate the website and handle inquiries. Where a referral is discussed, relevant details may be shared with independent local service providers to help them respond to the request. We will seek any additional permission required before doing so.

Independent providers are separate businesses. Their handling of information and their services are subject to their own practices and terms. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect rights, security or prevent misuse.

Retention and security

We retain request information for as long as needed to respond, manage relevant records and meet applicable obligations. The precise retention period depends on the purpose and the operator’s recordkeeping requirements. You can ask us about the retention of your inquiry at hello@example.com.

We use measures intended to protect information, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access to request messages should be limited to people who need it for the stated purposes.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your information, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern. The options available and how requests are handled may depend on applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

You can choose not to submit the form. Doing so will limit our ability to respond to a project inquiry through the website. Browser settings can also affect cookies, as explained in the Cookie Policy.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when the website or its information practices change. The date shown above identifies the latest update. For questions, contact hello@example.com.
